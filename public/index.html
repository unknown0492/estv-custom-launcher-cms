Display

Screen size and resolution of every box. A box can have its own setting (Boxes page, button). Boxes apply a change within 5 minutes.

Screen size

Smaller than 100 % shrinks the picture, so TVs that cut off the edges show everything. esTVLauncher's default is 94 %.

Resolution

Auto-start

The app every box opens by itself after start-up, and again when the home screen has been left alone. A box can have its own (Boxes page, button) or be changed in its Secret Menu; the newest change wins.

App

Timing

The box shows a countdown first; any remote key cancels it. A key press on the home screen restarts the wait, and the Secret Menu pauses it.

APK updates

Upload a new version of the launcher or of a button app. Boxes install the newest active APK of each app at their next check-in.
